Jeffrey Biegel, piano · Chelsea Tipton, Principal Guest Conductor · Greensboro Symphony Orchestra · Marquez – Danzón No. 9 · Boyer – Rhapsody in Red, White & Blue · Tchaikovsky – Symphony No. 4

The Greensboro Symphony Orchestra opens its season with a high-energy program featuring world-renowned pianist Jeffrey Biegel. The concert centers on the intersection of American heritage and grand Romantic drama.
