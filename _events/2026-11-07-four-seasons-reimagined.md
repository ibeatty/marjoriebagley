Simone Porter, violin · Christopher Dragon, Music Director · Greensboro Symphony Orchestra · Handel – Arrival of the Queen of Sheba · Mozart – Symphony No. 25 · Richter – The Four Seasons Recomposed

The Greensboro Symphony Orchestra welcomes violinist Simone Porter for a captivating program spanning three centuries of music. The concert opens with the bright elegance of George Frideric Handel’s Arrival of the Queen of Sheba, followed by the dramatic intensity of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart’s Symphony No. 25. Porter takes center stage in Max Richter’s The Four Seasons Reimagined, a mesmerizing modern reworking of Antonio Vivaldi’s beloved masterpiece.
