Christopher Dragon, Music Director · Greensboro Symphony Orchestra · Williams – Star Wars Suite for Orchestra · Holst – The Planets

Experience the iconic sounds of John Williams’ Star Wars Suite for Orchestra, bringing to life the adventure, heroism, and unforgettable themes from one of cinema’s most beloved sagas. Then, journey deeper into the universe with Gustav Holst’s The Planets, a thrilling and imaginative orchestral masterpiece that paints vivid portraits of the celestial bodies—from the fiery intensity of Mars to the ethereal beauty of Neptune.
