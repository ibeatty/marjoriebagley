Experience the music behind some of the most unforgettable films of our time. From The Lion King and Pirates of the Caribbean to Inception, the power scores of Hans Zimmer come to life in a thrilling, larger-than-life concert event with the Greensboro Symphony. A perfect night out for the whole family!
