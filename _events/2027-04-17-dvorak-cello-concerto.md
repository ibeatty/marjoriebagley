Julian Schwarz, cello · Christopher Dragon, Music Director · Greensboro Symphony Orchestra · Debussy – Petite Suite · Kodaly – Dances of Galanta · Dvorak – Cello Concerto

At the heart of the concert is Antonín Dvořák’s beloved Cello Concerto, one of the most powerful and lyrical works in the repertoire. Schwarz brings depth, virtuosity, and expressive intensity to this masterpiece, capturing both its sweeping drama and intimate beauty.
